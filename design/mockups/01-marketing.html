Marketing — contact sheet

Twenty frames of the public marketing site for a strength coach working online and in person in Northampton. Eight pages, each drawn at both widths — the desk at 1280 × 800 and the phone at 390 × 844 — plus four state screens. The site is one responsive web client, so each page's desk and phone frame sit together and the reflow is a decision, not an accident.

One conversion goal: book a free call. Every page points at /contact. Each frame carries one .btn.pri — the primary action — placed in the first viewport; every other call to action is a link or a secondary control. The shop and its "Buy" primary live on 02-shop.html; this sheet only teases the three ebooks and links across.

Vocabulary is frozen. Every frame is drawn in design/mockups/system.css and invents no class or colour of its own. The ground is dark, with one warm amber accent for the single thing that matters on a screen. Money renders as money — £9.99 — never a bare number. Status is a word plus a colour. Every empty state names the action that fills it, and every failure carries the button that recovers it.

Home

The screen that has to make the offer and the one call to action obvious: who Awad coaches, the three ways to train, how it works, proof, and a taste of the ebooks.

About

Who the coach is and why a lifter should trust him with their training, ending at the same call.

Online coaching

The remote offer in full: what's included, how a coached week runs, and the price, with the call as the only primary action.

In-person coaching

The Northampton offer: where, when, what a first session is, and the per-session price.

Testimonials

Proof in the lifters' own words, drawn with the awkward data the real page meets: a quote long enough to wrap and an honest, imperfect one.

FAQ

The questions a prospective client asks before booking, one answer drawn long enough to show real copy wrapping.

Contact

The one interactive page: the booking form, and what happens after you send it. This is where every other page points.

Legal

Privacy and terms in plain words, reachable from every footer. One primary action remains the call.

States

The empty, success, failure, and not-found screens the real site has to answer. Each carries a button that moves the visitor on.
