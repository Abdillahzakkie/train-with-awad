Shop — contact sheet

The sales surface: a three-ebook catalogue, one ebook in detail, the hand-off to hosted checkout, and what a buyer sees after they pay. Every screen is drawn at both widths — the laptop (1280 × 800) and the phone (390 × 844) — because the built site serves both from one responsive page.

Buy once, keep the file. Three guides: Strength Foundations £9.99, Eat to Perform £7.99, Train Anywhere £8.99. Money renders as money, with tabular figures so a price column aligns — never a bare number.

Vocabulary is frozen. Every frame is drawn in design/mockups/system.css and invents no class or colour of its own. The one primary action on a shop screen is Buy (ebook detail) or Download your ebook (order); everywhere else the site's single conversion goal, Book a free call, is the primary and the shop action is secondary.

The site never shows a card form. Checkout is hosted — the buyer enters card details on the payment provider's page, not ours. shop-checkout is a brief hand-off with no primary action; drawing a card field here would draw a screen the site does not own.
